      How to use the simulator

      Choose a mechanism above, drag to rotate the molecule, use the wheel to zoom in and move the progress bar to see every instant of the reaction.

      • Arrows: movement of electron pairs (curved arrows).
      • Orbitals: lobes of the participating orbitals (colors = phase; gold = lone pair).
      • Charges and cloud: δ⁻ / δ⁺ and electrostatic potential color (red = negative, blue = positive).
      • Measurements: live bond distances and angles.
      • The animation slows down by itself near each transition state [‡].
