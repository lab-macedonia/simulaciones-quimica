Conceptual route

Follow the sequence that connects hybridization, orbitals and geometry.

1

Hybridization

It determines how many atomic orbitals combine to produce hybrid orbitals.

2

Available p orbitals

The p orbitals that do not hybridize can take part in π bonds.

3

Overlap

Head-on overlap produces σ; side-by-side overlap produces π.

4

Geometry

sp³ → tetrahedral; sp² → trigonal planar; sp → linear.

5

Ideal angles

109.5° for sp³; 120° for sp²; 180° for sp.

6

Rotation

The presence of π bonds restricts rotation because it depends on the parallel orientation of p orbitals.

Teaching note

The orbital representation is a model. In real molecules electron densities are not rigid surfaces and angles may deviate from the ideal values.
