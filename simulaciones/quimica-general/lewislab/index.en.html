Resonance

What you should interpret

Resonance forms are not distinct molecules that alternate. They are contributors to a delocalized electronic structure.
Hypervalence: Lewis structures are useful for electron bookkeeping, but the modern explanation of hypervalent species does not require invoking a simple octet expansion through hybrid d orbitals.
