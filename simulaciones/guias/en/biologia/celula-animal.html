Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Identify organelles and their function.
  • Relate structure and function.
  • Group organelles into systems.

Prior knowledge

  • The cell as the basic unit.
  • Biological membranes.

Suggested activity

Path of a secretory protein

  1. Follow in the simulation nucleus → RER → Golgi → vesicle.
  2. Explain what happens at each step.
  3. Relate to insulin.
  4. Predict the effects of blocking the Golgi.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“The mitochondrion produces energy.”It converts the energy of nutrients into ATP.
“Ribosomes are only on the RER.”They are also free in the cytosol.
“The Golgi synthesizes proteins.”It modifies, sorts and ships them.

  4. ribosome

  1. lipid synthesis

  3. respiration
  4. photosynthesis

3. The smooth ER participates in…

  1. lipid synthesis
  2. synthesis of secreted proteins
  3. transcription
  4. mitosis

B. Essay

1. Describe the secretory pathway of a protein.

2. Explain why muscle cells have many mitochondria.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. B. lysosome — Intracellular digestion.
  2. B. organization of microtubules — It divides the cell.
  3. A. lipid synthesis — And detoxification.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. RER ribosome → ER lumen → vesicle → Golgi (modification) → secretory vesicle → exocytosis.
  2. High ATP demand for contraction.
