Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Identify structures exclusive to the plant cell.
  • Relate chloroplasts to photosynthesis.
  • Compare plant and animal cells.

Prior knowledge

  • Animal cell.
  • Basic photosynthesis.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“The cell wall is present in all cells.”It is in plants, fungi and bacteria, not in animals.
“Plants do not have mitochondria.”They do.
“The vacuole is only for storage.”It controls turgor and degrades.

B. Essay

1. Explain why plants need both mitochondria and chloroplasts.

2. Describe the function of the central vacuole.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. B. cellulose — Polysaccharide.
  2. B. connect neighboring cells — Cytoplasmic channels.
  3. B. becomes turgid — The wall prevents lysis.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. Chloroplasts fix light energy into carbohydrates; mitochondria oxidize them to obtain ATP.
  2. It maintains turgor, stores water, salts and pigments, and degrades macromolecules.
