Aromaticity and Hückel's rule

3D aromaticity simulator: Hückel's 4n + 2 rule, π molecular orbitals and the Frost circle, antiaromaticity, aromatic ions and heterocycles (pyrrole, pyridine, furan).

Open the simulation

⏱ 50 minLevel: undergraduate, early years11 multiple-choice questions · 2 essay questions

Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Apply the four criteria of aromaticity and the 4n + 2 rule.
  • Distinguish aromatic, antiaromatic and nonaromatic compounds.
  • Explain the acid–base behavior of cyclopentadiene and heterocycles from aromaticity.

Prior knowledge

  • sp² hybridization and p orbitals.
  • Resonance.

Suggested activity

Card sorting

  1. Hand out cards with benzene, cyclobutadiene, COT, cyclopentadienyl (anion and cation), tropylium, pyridine, pyrrole.
  2. Classify each one as aromatic, antiaromatic or nonaromatic.
  3. Check in the simulation by counting π electrons and MOs.
  4. Discuss the acidity of cyclopentadiene.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“Every ring with alternating double bonds is aromatic.”It must be planar, cyclic, conjugated and have 4n + 2 π electrons.
“COT is aromatic.”It has 8 π e⁻ and folds into a tub shape: nonaromatic.
“A heteroatom always contributes 2 e⁻.”It depends on its lone pair: in pyridine the pair is in the plane and contributes 0.

B. Essay

1. Justify the acidity of cyclopentadiene (pKa 16) compared with cyclopentene (>40).

2. Calculate the energy of the π MOs of benzene with α + 2βcos(2πk/6).

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. B. Benzene (6 π electrons) — 6 = 4(1) + 2.
  2. A. It is planar and cyclic with 4 π electrons (4n) — With 4n π electrons there are two unpaired electrons in degenerate nonbonding MOs.
  3. D. It folds into a tub shape to avoid antiaromaticity — With 8 π electrons a planar COT would be antiaromatic; it distorts and there is no complete cyclic conjugation.
  4. C. 6 — 4 from the two C=C + 2 from the carbanion lone pair = 6: aromatic.
  5. B. In pyrrole the N pair is part of the π sextet; in pyridine it is in the plane — Protonating the N of pyrrole would destroy the aromatic sextet; the pyridine pair (sp²) does not participate.
  6. A. Tropylium cation (C₇H₇⁺) — Tropylium has 6 π electrons in a planar, fully conjugated ring.
  7. D. 3 — There is one lowest-energy MO and two degenerate ones, all bonding; the 6 electrons fill them.
  8. C. Its conjugate base is aromatic (6 π electrons) — Deprotonation of the CH₂ generates the cyclopentadienyl anion, which is aromatic and highly stabilized.
  9. B. 6 — Seven p orbitals with a + charge: 6 e⁻.
  10. B. antiaromatic — 4n e⁻ in a planar ring.
  11. C. 2 e⁻ — A lone pair to the π system: 4 + 2 = 6.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. Its conjugate base, the cyclopentadienyl anion, is aromatic (6 π e⁻).
  2. k = 0: α + 2β; k = ±1: α + β; k = ±2: α − β; k = 3: α − 2β. Six e⁻ fill the three bonding MOs.
