Thin-layer and column chromatography

TLC plate and silica column in 3D: polarity of compounds and eluent, retention factor Rf, resolution between spots and column elution chromatogram.

Open the simulation

⏱ 40 minLevel: undergraduate, early years8 multiple-choice questions · 2 essay questions

Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Calculate Rf and interpret a plate.
  • Choose an eluent according to polarity.
  • Predict the elution order on a column.

Prior knowledge

  • Polarity of molecules.
  • Intermolecular forces.

Simulation walkthrough

Use the lesson (bottom player) for the guided walkthrough and the Lab tab to explore freely; “Back to the lesson” resumes the script.

#StageCore idea
1Stationary phase and mobile phaseSilica (polar) retains polar compounds more; the eluent carries them along
2Thin-layer plate (TLC)Rf = distance of the compound / distance of the solvent front
3Choosing the eluentMore polar → higher Rf. Adjust until the spots separate
4Chromatographic columnThey separate into bands that come out in order of increasing polarity
5Resolution and purityTwo spots separate well if ΔRf is large and the spots are small

Suggested activity

Optimize the eluent

  1. With a mixture of three compounds, try hexane, a 9:1 mixture and a 1:1 mixture with ethyl acetate.
  2. Record the Rf values.
  3. Choose the system that gives ΔRf > 0.15.
  4. Predict the order on the column.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“The most polar compound rises the most.”On silica the most polar is retained most (lowest Rf).
“Rf can be greater than 1.”It is between 0 and 1.
“The baseline is marked with an ink pen.”A graphite pencil is used; ink migrates.

B. Essay

1. A spot has Rf = 0.05 in hexane/EtOAc 9:1. What would you change?

2. Explain why it is advisable to use a plate with a UV indicator.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. D. nonpolar — It interacts least with the silica.
  2. C. d compound / d front — Always between 0 and 1.
  3. B. not polar enough; increase it — It cannot displace the compounds from the silica.
  4. A. 0,3 y 0,5 — There is room on both sides.
  5. D. elutes last — It is retained the most.
  6. B. increases Rf — It moves the compounds further.
  7. B. 2 cm — 0,4 × 5.
  8. A. polar — It is adsorbed more strongly.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. Increase the proportion of ethyl acetate (more polar eluent) until Rf is 0.3–0.5.
  2. It allows conjugated compounds to be visualized without a destructive stain.
