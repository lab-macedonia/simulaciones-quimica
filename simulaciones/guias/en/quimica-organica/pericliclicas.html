Electrocyclic and Sigmatropic Reactions

Pericyclic reactions beyond Diels–Alder: 4-, 6- and 8-electron electrocyclizations (conrotatory and disrotatory), frontier orbitals, thermal and photochemical control, [1,5] H shifts and the [3,3] Cope rearrangement with Woodward–Hoffmann rules.

Open the simulation

⏱ 50 minLevel: undergraduate, early years8 multiple-choice questions · 2 essay questions

Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Apply the Woodward–Hoffmann rules to electrocyclizations.
  • Predict the stereochemistry of the product according to con/disrotation.
  • Recognize [1,5] and [3,3] sigmatropic reactions.

Prior knowledge

  • π molecular orbitals of polyenes.
  • Diels–Alder.

Suggested activity

Con- or disrotatory

  1. For trans,trans- and cis,trans-2,4-hexadiene, predict the thermal product.
  2. Repeat with light.
  3. Check with the rotations in the simulation.
  4. Build a 4n/4n+2 × Δ/hν table.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“Light changes the type of reaction.”It changes the allowed mode of rotation.
“Dis- and conrotatory give the same product.”They give different diastereomers.
“Cope requires a catalyst.”It is thermal and concerted.

B. Essay

1. Predict the product of the thermal cyclization of (2E,4Z)-hexa-2,4-diene.

2. Explain the 4n/4n+2 rule.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. D. conrotatory — 4 e⁻, thermal: conrotatory.
  2. C. disrotatory — 4n + 2 thermal: disrotatory.
  3. B. disrotatory — The selection is reversed.
  4. A. [3,3] sigmatropic — The C3–C4 σ bond migrates to C1–C6.
  5. D. trans-3,4-dimethylcyclobutene — Conrotatory rotation, E,E groups → trans.
  6. B. disrotatory — 4n+2 thermal → dis.
  7. B. suprafacial — Thermally allowed suprafacial.
  8. A. con — 8 e⁻ = 4n thermal → con.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. 2,4-Hexadiene has 4 π e⁻, so thermal cyclization is conrotatory. The (E,Z) isomer gives cis-3,4-dimethylcyclobutene and the (E,E) gives the trans.
  2. It depends on the symmetry of the HOMO: 4n e⁻ thermal, con; 4n+2 thermal, dis; light promotes an e⁻ and changes the HOMO.
