Mass spectrometry and ¹³C NMR

3D simulator of mass spectrometry (molecular ion, Cl and Br isotope pattern, α-cleavage, McLafferty) and ¹³C NMR (non-equivalent carbons, chemical shift) to solve organic structures.

Open the simulation

⏱ 55 minLevel: undergraduate, early years11 multiple-choice questions · 2 essay questions

Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Determine molar mass and halogens from the molecular ion and isotopic patterns.
  • Interpret typical fragmentations.
  • Use the number and position of ¹³C signals to choose between isomers.

Prior knowledge

  • Isotopes and atomic mass.
  • Chemical equivalence and symmetry.

Suggested activity

Isomers and spectra

  1. Hand out the C₅H₁₀O isomers: pentan-2-one, pentan-3-one and 3-methylbutanal.
  2. Predict the number of ¹³C signals for each.
  3. Use the MS base peak to tell them apart.
  4. Confirm with the simulation.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“M⁺• is always the highest peak.”The base peak (100 %) may be a fragment; M⁺• may be small or absent.
“The ¹³C intensity is proportional to the number of C.”In decoupled ¹³C the intensity is not quantitative.
«Cl da M y M+2 1:1».Cl da 3:1; Br da 1:1.

B. Essay

1. A compound with M = 122/124 (1:1) and two ¹³C signals. Propose the structure.

2. Explain the α-fragmentation of propanone.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. C. an odd number of N — Nitrogen rule.
  2. B. Cl — ³⁵Cl:³⁷Cl ≈ 3:1.
  3. A. CH₃C≡O⁺ — α-cleavage of the C–C bond next to the C=O.
  4. D. 3 signals — Two equivalent CH₃ and two equivalent CH₂, plus the C=O.
  5. C. C=O of ketone or aldehyde — Aldehyde/ketone carbonyls: 190–220 ppm.
  6. B. an H at γ to the C=O — Transfer of an H at γ through a 6-membered state.
  7. A. is not proportional to the no. of C — It depends on relaxation and the NOE effect.
  8. D. 1:1 — ⁷⁹Br and ⁸¹Br are almost equally probable.
  9. C. 5 — CH₃, ipso C, ortho (×2), meta (×2) and para: 5 distinct signals.
  10. A. an odd number of N — Nitrogen rule.
  11. D. 200 ppm — 190–220 ppm.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. The 1:1 pattern indicates one Br. With M = 122/124 the formula is C₃H₇Br. 1-Bromopropane would give 3 ¹³C signals; 2-bromopropane, 2 (two equivalent CH₃ and one CH). It is 2-bromopropane.
  2. Cleavage of the C–C bond next to the C=O: CH₃CO⁺ (m/z 43, base peak) + CH₃•.
