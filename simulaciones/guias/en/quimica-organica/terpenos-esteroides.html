Terpenes and Steroids

The isoprene rule in 3D: linear monoterpenes, sesquiterpenes and diterpenes with their C₅ units, squalene cyclization and the steroid skeleton (rings A–D, numbering) with cholesterol, testosterone, estradiol and progesterone.

Open the simulation

⏱ 45 minLevel: undergraduate, early years8 multiple-choice questions · 2 essay questions

Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Identify isoprene units in terpenes.
  • Classify terpenes by number of C₅ units.
  • Describe the steroid skeleton and its relation to hormones.

Prior knowledge

  • Alkenes and nomenclature.
  • Cyclohexane chair.

5Steroid hormonesSmall changes in the substituents change the biological function

Suggested activity

Counting isoprenes

  1. Limonene, menthol, farnesol and cholesterol are given.
  2. Mark the isoprene units (head-to-tail).
  3. Classify monoterpenes, sesquiterpenes, triterpenes.
  4. Compare testosterone and estradiol in the simulation.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“Cholesterol is a 27-C terpene that comes directly from isoprene.”It is formed from squalene (30 C) and loses three methyls.
“All steroids are hormones.”They include cholesterol, bile acids, vitamin D.
“The rings of the steroid are flat.”They are in chair form and trans-fused.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. B. isoprene (C₅) — Isoprene rule.
  2. A. 15 carbons — 3 units × 5.
  3. D. squalene — Squalene → lanosterol → cholesterol.
  4. C. 3 six-membered rings and 1 five-membered — A, B, C six-membered; D five-membered.
  5. B. aromatic A ring — It loses the C19 methyl and aromatizes.
  6. C. 20 C — Four isoprene units.
  7. A. 4 rings (3 six-membered and 1 five-membered) — Cyclopentanoperhydrophenanthrene.
  8. A. lanosterol — Cyclization to lanosterol.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. Terpenes are multiples of C₅ (2-methylbuta-1,3-diene joined head-to-tail); e.g., geraniol is two units (C₁₀).
  2. Estradiol has an aromatic A ring (phenol) and no C19 methyl; testosterone has an enone in A and two angular methyls.
