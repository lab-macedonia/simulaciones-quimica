Entropy and Gibbs Free Energy

3D simulator of entropy and Gibbs free energy: microstates and S = k·ln W, spontaneous expansion, entropy of the states of matter, ΔG = ΔH − TΔS, equilibrium temperature and relation to K.

Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Relate entropy to microstates.
  • Predict spontaneity with ΔG = ΔH − TΔS.
  • Link ΔG° and K.

Prior knowledge

  • Thermochemistry.
  • Logarithms.

#StageCore idea
1Microstates and macrostatesThe same macroscopic distribution can be achieved in many ways
2S = k·ln WEntropy: a measure of the number of accessible microstates
3Spontaneous expansionA gas fills all the available volume: ΔS = nR ln(V₂/V₁) > 0
4States of matterS(s) < S(l) ≪ S(g): more freedom of motion, more microstates
5ΔG = ΔH − TΔSCombines the effect of the system (ΔH) and the surroundings (ΔS_surr = −ΔH/T)
6ΔG, K and equilibriumΔG° = −RT ln K: a single number summarizes the equilibrium

Suggested activity

Four cases of spontaneity

  1. Classify reactions according to the signs of ΔH and ΔS.
  2. Calculate the crossover temperature in each case.
  3. Check with the simulation.
  4. Discuss why ice melts spontaneously above 0 °C.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“Spontaneous means fast.”Spontaneity is thermodynamic; rate is kinetic.
“Entropy always increases in the system.”The entropy of the universe increases; that of the system may decrease.
“ΔG° < 0 implies there are no reactants.”It implies K > 1, not complete reaction.

B. Essay

1. ΔH = +40 kJ and ΔS = +100 J/K: at what T does it become spontaneous?

2. Calculate K for ΔG° = −17.1 kJ/mol at 298 K.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. D. 184 756 — C(20,10) = 184 756.
  2. C. S = k ln W — S = k_B ln W.
  3. B. R ln 2 — ΔS = nR ln(V₂/V₁).
  4. A. g > l > s — More freedom of motion = more microstates.
  5. D. at low temperature — ΔG = ΔH − TΔS; the −TΔS term becomes positive at high T.
  6. C. T > ΔH/ΔS — As T rises the −TΔS term gains weight.
  7. B. 10 — ln K = −ΔG°/RT = 2.3; K ≈ 10.
  8. A. ΔG = 0 — At equilibrium ΔG = 0 (and Q = K).
  9. A. spontaneous at all T — ΔG always negative.
  10. B. < 1 — ΔG° = −RT ln K.
  11. D. water vapor — Greater freedom.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. T > ΔH/ΔS = 40000/100 = 400 K.
  2. K = exp(17100/(8.314×298)) = e^6.9 ≈ 1 × 10³.
