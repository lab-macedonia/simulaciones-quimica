Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Draw Lewis structures with minimal formal charges.
  • Identify resonance and its effect on bond length.
  • Recognize exceptions to the octet rule.

Prior knowledge

  • Valence electrons.
  • Covalent bond.

Suggested activity

Best Lewis structure

  1. Draw SO₂, NO₃⁻ and SF₆.
  2. Calculate formal charges.
  3. Compare with the simulation.
  4. Choose the best structure and justify.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“All atoms obey the octet.”There are exceptions: BF₃, SF₆, NO.
“Resonance is an equilibrium between structures.”It is a single hybrid structure.
“Formal charge is the real charge.”It is a counting formalism.

B. Essay

1. Draw ozone and explain why both O–O bonds are equal.

2. Calculate the formal charge of each atom in CO.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. B. +1 — 5 − (0 + 4).
  2. C. 3 — Three positions for the double bond.
  3. B. an incomplete octet — Six electrons on B.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. Two resonance structures with single and double bonds interchanged; the hybrid has a bond order of 1.5.
  2. With a triple bond and a lone pair on each atom: C −1, O +1.
