Molecular Orbitals in Diatomics

Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Build MO diagrams of homonuclear diatomics.
  • Calculate the bond order.
  • Predict magnetism.

Prior knowledge

  • Electron configuration.
  • Atomic s and p orbitals.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“The antibonding MO has no electrons.”It can be occupied; it subtracts from the bond order.
“For all period-2 diatomics the MO order is the same.”Up to N₂ the σ2p lies above π2p (s–p mixing).
“Paramagnetic = has a charge.”It means unpaired electrons.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. C. 3 — (8 − 2)/2 = 3.
  2. B. it has 2 unpaired e⁻ in π* — The two degenerate π*₂ₚ each receive one electron (Hund).
  3. A. OE = 0 — σ² σ*²: equal number of bonding and antibonding.
  4. D. a nodal plane between the nuclei — The subtraction of phases produces a node between the nuclei and higher energy.
  5. C. two pairs in π (BO = 2) — C₂: σ₂ₛ²σ*₂ₛ²π⁴ → BO = 2, with both pairs in π orbitals.
  6. B. decreases — One e⁻ is removed from π*: BO 2 → 2.5.
  7. A. π MO — Side-by-side overlap produces π MOs.
  8. D. 6 — The number of MOs equals the number of AOs combined.
  9. B. 2 — (8 − 4)/2.
  10. C. O₂ — Two unpaired e⁻ in π*.
  11. A. shorter — OE = 2,5.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. BO = (8 − 8)/2 = 0: it does not exist.
  2. In C₂ the π2p MOs fill before σ2p, with BO = 2.
