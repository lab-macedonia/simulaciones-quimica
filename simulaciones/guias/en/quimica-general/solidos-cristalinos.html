Crystalline solids and unit cells

3D simulator of crystalline solids: simple, body-centered and face-centered cubic cells, close packing hcp and ccp, ionic structures (NaCl, CsCl, ZnS, CaF₂), atoms per cell, coordination number, packing fraction and density.

Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Count atoms per cell.
  • Calculate packing fraction and coordination.
  • Obtain the density from the cell.

Prior knowledge

  • Mole and molar mass.
  • Cube geometry.

B. Essay

1. Calculate the density of bcc Fe (a = 287 pm, M = 55.85 g/mol).

2. Explain the ABAB sequence versus ABCABC.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. A. 4 — 8·⅛ + 6·½ = 4.
  2. D. 8 — Each atom touches 8 neighbors, those at the corners of the cube.
  3. C. a = 2√2·r — The contact is along the face diagonal: 4r = a√2.
  4. B. 74 % — fcc and hcp: π/(3√2) ≈ 0.740.
  5. A. ccp (fcc) — The third layer occupies the holes that the first does not cover.
  6. D. 6 : 6 — Each ion is surrounded by 6 counterions in octahedral holes.
  7. C. Z·M / (N_A·V) — Mass of the cell / volume of the cell.
  8. B. 1 CsCl unit — 8·⅛ Cl⁻ + 1 Cs⁺ at the center.
  9. B. 2 — 8 × 1/8 + 1.
  10. C. 2√2 r — The face diagonal is 4r.
  11. C. 12 — Close packing.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. ρ = 2 × 55,85/(6,022×10²³ × (2,87×10⁻⁸)³) = 7,85 g/cm³.
  2. ABAB = hcp; ABCABC = ccp (fcc). Different stacking, same 74 %.
