Inorganic Nomenclature

Formulas and names of ionic compounds in 3D: charge balance, Stock, systematic and traditional nomenclature, halogen oxoanions, acids and oxoacids, with ion assembly.

Open the simulation

⏱ 40 minLevel: undergraduate, early years9 multiple-choice questions · 2 essay questions

Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Write formulas by charge balance.
  • Name using Stock, systematic and traditional nomenclature.
  • Recognize oxoanions and oxoacids.

Prior knowledge

  • Periodic table and ionic charges.
  • Oxidation states.

#StageCore idea
1Ions and charge balanceAn ionic compound is neutral: Σ(+ charges) = Σ(− charges)
2Stock nomenclaturecation + anion; the oxidation state goes in Roman numerals if there are several
3Systematic and traditionalPrefixes mono-, di-, tri-… and suffixes -ous / -ic
4Halogen oxoanionshypo- … -ite < -ite < -ate < per- … -ate according to the number of oxygens
5Acids, oxoacids and hydroxides-ide → hydro-…-ic acid ; -ate → -ic acid ; -ite → -ous acid

Suggested activity

Three nomenclatures

  1. Present 8 formulas (Fe₂O₃, SO₃, HClO₄, Cu(OH)₂, NaHCO₃…).
  2. Name them in the three nomenclatures.
  3. Check it in the simulation.
  4. Discuss which one IUPAC prefers.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“Subscripts are charges.”They are ratios of atoms or ions.
“Fe₂O₃ is iron(II) oxide.”It is iron(III): Fe³⁺.
“Cl⁻ from hydrochloric acid and ClO⁻ are the same.”Chloride versus hypochlorite.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. A. Al₂O₃ — Crisscross: Al³⁺ and O²⁻.
  2. D. iron(III) chloride — Fe³⁺.
  3. C. chlorate — 3 O: -ate.
  4. B. sulfurous — -ite → -ous.
  5. A. calcium hydroxide — Cation + OH⁻.
  6. D. diiron trioxide — Multiplicative prefixes.
  7. B. copper(II) oxide — Cu²⁺.
  8. B. nitric — Nitrate anion.
  9. B. Na₂SO₄ — 2(+1) + (−2).

Essay (marking guide)

  1. Stock: iron(III) oxide; systematic: diiron trioxide; traditional: ferric oxide.
  2. Ca(ClO)₂.
