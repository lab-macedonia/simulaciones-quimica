Lenses and the human eye

3D optics simulator: rays through a converging lens, real inverted images, the magnifying glass, the thin-lens equation, the eye and accommodation, myopia and hyperopia with their corrective lenses.

Open the simulation

⏱ 45 minLevel: Grade 98 multiple-choice questions · 2 essay questions

Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Use rays to construct the image formed by a converging lens.
  • Apply the thin-lens equation and magnification.
  • Explain how the eye works, accommodation and the correction of myopia and hyperopia.

Prior knowledge

  • Light travels in straight lines.
  • Working with fractions.

Suggested activity

Design glasses for a classmate

  1. With the short-sighted eye, try lenses from −1 to −5 D until distant vision is sharp.
  2. With the long-sighted eye, do the same for an object at 25 cm.
  3. Compare your result with the «Suggested corrective lens» in the Calculation tab.
  4. Explain why a prescription says “−3.25 D” and what the sign means.

Common misconceptions

MisconceptionCorrection
“The image on the retina is upright.”It is inverted; the brain interprets it as upright.
“A magnifier enlarges any object at any distance.”It only gives a virtual magnified image if the object is within the focal length.
“Short-sighted people wear magnifying lenses.”They wear diverging lenses (negative power).

B. Essay

1. A 2 cm object is 15 cm from a lens with f = 10 cm. Calculate the position, size and type of image.

2. Explain why older people hold the newspaper further away to read.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. C. pass through the focus — That is the definition of the focus.
  2. B. 15 cm — 1/dᵢ = 1/10 − 1/30 = 2/30.
  3. D. virtual, upright and larger — That is how a magnifying glass works.
  4. D. real and inverted — The eye is a converging system; the brain interprets the image.
  5. C. increasing the power of the lens to see up close — The ciliary muscles make the lens rounder.
  6. C. far away — The image of distant things forms in front of the retina.
  7. A. diverging lenses — Negative power: they move the focus back to the retina.
  8. A. 50 cm — f = 1/P = 0.5 m.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. dᵢ = 30 cm, m = −2: real, inverted image, 4 cm tall.
  2. With presbyopia the lens no longer accommodates enough; moving the object away means less power is needed to focus it.
