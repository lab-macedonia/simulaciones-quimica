Learning objectives

By the end, the student will be able to:

  • Order planets by size, distance and period.
  • Apply Kepler's third law.
  • Compare weight and mass on different bodies.

Prior knowledge

  • Gravitation and orbits.
  • Proportionality and powers.

Suggested activity

Verify Kepler

  1. Record a and T for four planets.
  2. Calculate T²/a³.
  3. Check that it is constant in AU and year units.
  4. Predict T for a body with a = 4 AU.

Common mistakes

MisconceptionCorrection
“Planets move at constant speed.”They move faster at perihelion.
“Mass and weight are the same.”Mass is constant; weight depends on g.
“Pluto is a planet.”It has been a dwarf planet since 2006.

B. Essay

1. Calculate the weight of a 60 kg person on Mars (g = 3.7 m/s²).

2. Calculate the period of an asteroid with a = 2.77 AU.

Answer key

Multiple choice

  1. B. 8 years — T = a^1,5.
  2. B. Jupiter — Diameter ≈ 11 times Earth's.
  3. B. v is greater at perihelion — Equal areas in equal times.

Essay (marking guide)

  1. W = mg = 60 × 3.7 = 222 N (≈ 22.6 kgf).
  2. T = 2.77^1.5 = 4.6 years.
